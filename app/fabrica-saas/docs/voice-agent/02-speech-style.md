# ADV-11 Speech Style & Humanness

## HumanSpeechStyle
- 10 anti-patterns (bulletted lists, long sentences, jargon)
- 8 good patterns (short responses, natural fillers)
- MAX_VOICE_RESPONSE_WORDS: SIMPLE=15, NORMAL=30, COMPLEX=50, NEVER=100

## NaturalDisfluencyPolicy
- 10 allowed disfluencies: "pues", "bueno", "a ver"
- 5 forbidden: "como… como…" (repeated filler), unnatural artifacts

## VoiceAgentProfile
- 6 personality types: FRIENDLY, PROFESSIONAL, EMPATHETIC, ENERGETIC, CONSULTATIVE, CALM
- 4 speaking styles: CONVERSATIONAL, PROFESSIONAL, EMPATHETIC, DIRECT
- 3 speech rates: SLOW, NORMAL, FAST
- 3 formality levels: FORMAL, SEMIFORMAL, INFORMAL
